About

WLAN Pi is a community-driven, open-source Wi-Fi analysis tool built on single-board computers. Wireless professionals use it to scan, test, monitor, and troubleshoot wired and wireless networks. This includes capturing packets, profiling clients, measuring throughput, and running speed tests.
